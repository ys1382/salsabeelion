Shelf camera coach

Range: one section of shelf (~3–4 ft wide), about 3–5 feet away for normal paperbacks — not across the room, and not pressed into the books. Small or dark spines may need a step closer. On-screen hints say when the frame is ready. Photos are not saved — discarded right after titles are read. Reads need a visible title and author; blocked or half-hidden spines are flagged, not guessed.
