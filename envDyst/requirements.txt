A 2D, story-driven game set in a world that closely resembles our own, where the player gradually realizes that something is off—not because the world suddenly changed, but because they’ve begun to see it more clearly. The game opens with a short prologue in which everything feels normal, with full color and rich, layered sound, but after a key realization about how environmental systems actually work, the player returns to the same world and experiences it differently: colors are subtly muted, sounds feel slightly muffled and thinner, and the environment feels less alive, even as people continue their everyday routines. Through exploration and small, meaningful actions—restoring parks, influencing systems, and understanding connections—the player gradually brings back richness to the world, with clearer sound, more vibrant visuals, and spaces that feel genuinely peaceful. Rather than presenting a distant or dramatic dystopia, the game reveals a quiet, present-day one, using atmosphere and perception to show how everyday systems shape the world, and how thoughtful, sustained change can make it feel alive again.
