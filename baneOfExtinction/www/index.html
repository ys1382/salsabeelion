Bane of Extinction

Walk, notice, and learn real wildlife into a codex — without bothering the living neighborhood.

Owner-only beta. Not public. Camera scan works on phone or tablet (same idea as Halalit scanners). Desktop is for the wildlife codex.

Open wildlife codex Neighborhood missions Camera scan (phone)

What’s ready

  • Level 1 — phone camera scan → species guess + codex art
  • Wildlife codex fact book (learned facts on your account; fact levels separate from missions)
  • Looking-at places (favorites on this device) — no GPS; facts follow the place you choose
  • Level 2 — beginning missions after ~15 finds (signature signs; ant mound full visual)
  • Level 3 — after a few Level 2 missions: same organism, different place meaning (healthy / meh / warning) + under-the-hood landscape stories
  • Don’t get too close — no scan or quest needs a risky close-up; use path distance or signs for animals that can bite or surprise you
  • Coming later: walk sessions, buddy, Trail Guide
