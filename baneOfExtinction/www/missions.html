Level 1 = scans (meet the neighbor). Level 2 = beginning missions (homes, signs, care). Level 3 = place meanings & under-the-hood landscape stories — not tourist postcard facts. No GPS. Don’t get too close.

Don’t get too close. No quest needs a risky close-up. A calm yard squirrel a few feet away is one thing; crowding a possum, raccoon, skunk, or anything that can bite or surprise you is another. Stay back, use the path, or scan signs (mounds, feathers, holes) instead.
