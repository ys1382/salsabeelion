Every pair of lists meets in its own square. A clue like “the mountain animal hunts blue sheep” is marked where mountains meets blue sheep — you may not know the species yet. Tap a cell for a red X, tap again to lock the match, tap again to clear. The board does not copy marks onto other squares for you. Tap a clue when you are done with it: one pen line, and it drops to the bottom if it was not already last.
