Logic grid

Owner-only draft. Species most people never stand next to — still keystones, still threatened. Matching teaches why they still touch a world you live in.

This page is only for the site owner.

The board is an L: every pair of different lists meets in one square. Blank space is not a square — a list does not meet itself. A “did not” is an X where those two labels meet. A clue that ties two traits (kelp coast and oil spills) is marked where those two meet — you may not know the species yet. Tap a cell for a red X, tap again to clear. Double-tap to lock a match (green circle). The rest of that row and column in the same little square get red Xs. Other little squares stay as you left them — a lock on prey × place does not fill the species list. Tap a clue when you are done with it: one pen line, and it drops to the bottom if it was not already last.
