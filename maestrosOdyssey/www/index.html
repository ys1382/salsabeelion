Monday · café day 1
Learning card: 200 pesos

How to play

  • Move — arrow keys or WASD
  • Enter café — walk to the door and step through
  • R — read the drink menu, strike board, and house rules (wall behind the counter)
  • E — talk to Mara (inside the café; read the menu first to practice ordering)
  • T — stand on a chair or stool to sit; press T again to stand
  • D / F — while seated, sip your drink (D) or eat (F) after you pick up your order

During your visit

  • Weekday — top center shows the fiction day (Monday first); one completed visit per day
  • Menu — new drinks and food unlock as the week advances
  • Finish your order — sit and finish your drink and/or food before the visit is done
  • Restart (top left) — fresh Mara intro, café week, and learning-card balance in this tab
  • Space, E, or R — close what you’re reading
