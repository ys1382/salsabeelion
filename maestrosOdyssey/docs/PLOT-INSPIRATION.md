## 2026-10-11

**Outline:** Child who grew up segregated is sent to the village to avoid becoming desensitized. They return as an ambassador in training, attempting to stop a war.

**One way to complicate that:** They come to segregate the people, but their actions result in further conflict as people are forced to deal with one another. No wars yet, but not what the player was expecting either.

**When they leave the village:** The protagonist has a team. Everyone thinks they are on the same page, but they have different ideas of how to achieve peace.

1. Someone thinks desegregation will actively lead to war, because people have spent too much time apart, and being forced to deal with one another will just dredge up old grudges.
2. Another person agrees with desegregation, but does not trust the peoples to get along unless the beginning is something underhanded — people manipulated with the best of intentions, like Dumbledore with Harry Potter.
3. A third is naive and overestimates a lighthearted approach: everyone will just get along immediately because of a potluck, or something similar.
4. A fourth tries what might normally be the right approach, but between two parties who are as close to civil war as you can get — like trawlers and merfolk.
5. A fifth jumps in without a plan and sees how it goes, and lands in hot water because their “daredevil but harmless” approach is with a group or people who already hate the human race as a whole.

**Later:** This initial team ends up leading their own different factions, one way or another, and the team fights one another. Not a bloody skirmish — a very definite conflict, like how the fellowship in *The Hobbit* was split after Bilbo stole that fourteenth of the treasure.

**Blurb (from the notes above):**

Raised apart from every other people, a child is sent to a village so they will not grow numb. They come back an ambassador in training, meant to stop a war by keeping the peoples separate.

There is no war waiting. The work forces neighbors to face one another, and the quiet starts to crack.

The team beside them all thinks they want the same peace. One would keep the distance, sure that old grudges only need a reason. One would mix the peoples, but only by steering them — a kindness with a hidden hand. One trusts a shared meal to settle it by morning. One tries the honest path between trawlers and merfolk, two sides already as close to civil war as peace allows. One walks in with no plan, daring and harmless, toward people who already hate humankind.

Those friends do not stay a team. Each comes to lead a different side. The break is final, and nobody has to bleed for it to be a fight.

**Even later:** Some of the factions, following leaders who each used to be part of the protagonist's team, attack other factions. It does not turn into a world war. It gets bad, without casualties.

## 2026-10-11 — separate branch

Probably separate from the team story above.

The segregation is secretly not because of the wars. Everyone thinks it is because of the wars. If it had been because of war, more people would have come to their senses instead of settling on segregation as the only way to maintain the peace.

Something even more dangerous than all those world wars is deliberately splitting the peoples apart and keeping them apart, to leave them defenseless to protect their world.
