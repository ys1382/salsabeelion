## Dragon’s Brew — house rules

### Food & drink (fixed sign — not refusal bullets)

- No **customer** (sentient / people) blood served here.
- No **bovine** blood served here.
- No **lizard** or **cow** meat.
- Fish is fine; **not listed** (goes without saying).

### Refuse service (bullet list after header)

**We reserve the right to refuse service due to**

- Racism
- Predator-Attitude
- Fighting
- Loud Arguments
- Smoking
- Drinking Alcohol
- And picking on customers for any not-aforementioned reasons.

### Strike of the week (erasable board)

One ingredient off menu when sourcing is unethical (e.g. white sugar on strike → brown sugar until the lot is acceptable). World moves week to week.

### Blood economy (city — not all on the wall)

- Blood is drawn with **tech** at clinics / co-ops — **not** by vampires feeding in the café.
- **Vampire cafés elsewhere:** human blood allowed — **donation-only**, paid, ethical; has helped people in poverty. Scream Street–style **supply as utility** (routes, lines, pouches) — drama from logistics, not horror.
- **Dragon’s Brew:** no customer blood, no bovine blood — **inclusive house rule**, not a judgment that ethical bovine or sentient donation is “evil” citywide. Minotaurs may donate bovine blood upstream; serving it **here** puts the room on edge.
- Evening animal blood at Dragon’s Brew: non-bovine, non-sentient (e.g. goat, sheep), herd-health draws, sealed pouches.
